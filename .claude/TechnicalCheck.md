Perform an independent review of the current project state.

Ignore prior conversations, historical issues, redesign discussions, and previous assumptions. Evaluate only the current repository, current documentation, and the latest execution results.

Review the entire project as it exists today and provide an objective assessment covering:

What the system is capable of today
Which components appear mature and stable
Which components appear incomplete or experimental
Architectural strengths
Architectural weaknesses
Technical debt
Scalability concerns
Maintainability concerns
Areas that appear over-engineered
Areas that appear under-engineered
The highest-value next engineering tasks

Do not organize the review around project phases.

Instead, evaluate the project from the perspective of a Game Technical Director reviewing a working forensic platform.

For every major conclusion:

Provide evidence from the current codebase or execution results
Explain why the conclusion was reached
Estimate implementation complexity if changes are recommended

At the end, answer the following questions directly:

What is the current maturity level of the project?
Is the architecture moving in a sustainable direction?
What are the three most important technical risks?
What are the three most important opportunities?
If development stopped today, what would be the strongest portfolio-worthy aspects of the project?
If you were the lead engineer, what would you prioritize next?

Focus on the current state only.

Do not evaluate based on historical failures, old assumptions, or previous design discussions.
